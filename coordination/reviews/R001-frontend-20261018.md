## Limits and required follow-up

- No device deployment or live WindowServer acceptance. The provider has not been loaded into the Ventura 22F82 process, and local SkyLight symbol resolution has not been proven against a live image. Treat the change as an offline capability repair, not input success.
- The full suite is not green: 708 tests, 14 failures, 5 errors and 14 skips, with documented baseline families. This is correctly reported and must remain so.
- `MSHookMessageEx` validates the presence of an encoding, not exact ABI equality; callers remain responsible for compatible signatures. The provider still lacks a general thread-quiescence protocol. These limits are acceptable for this packet but must not be hidden behind an `INSTALLED` marker.
- Exact route classification is a contract; it does not prove an AppKit window exists, a CGS post succeeds, or pixels/input are visible. Those require the backend's device generation and real endpoint evidence.
