Date: 2026-10-18 Asia/Hong_Kong. Candidate `c97e219` plus the one-file
`mac_hooks.m` signing patch.

R012 is accepted as a major bounded milestone. The clean package build and
real archive verification passed; the installed arm64e callsite is byte
verified as `pacia1716` with zero modifier followed by `blraaz`; provider
publish/trust and the original-callable method probe pass. The owned
generation survived the former failure: tccd-system/authd/systemstatusd

service log line is absent, and the arm64 interopd symbol-not-found line is a
different architecture/path. Treat the callsite bytes plus survival as strong
regression evidence, not a direct function-entry witness.

The remaining acceptance boundary is explicit: final composite is still
`missing producer=0 sequence=0`, no pixels were claimed, TCC was not
re-tested, input remains the prior 1002 baseline, and R016 reopen freshness
was not run. The 15 EXC_GUARD reports are a separate unclassified delta and
must not be conflated with the fixed XPC crash. The publisher temporary
Identifier remains a reproducibility issue, not the explanation for this
milestone's startup recovery.
