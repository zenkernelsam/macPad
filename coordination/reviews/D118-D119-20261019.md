## D118 backend: CHANGES_REQUESTED

Candidate remains offline and PENDING. The D118 fix correctly closes the
reported drain-vs-fd-mutation race: `D113_FDMUT` is atomically claimed while
checking the active drainer count, and `admit()` rejects the phase. The
reported L13/L14 tests exercise those interleavings. However, the new
`fdmut_claim()` omits `D113_CLOSED` from its rejection mask, contradicting
`d113_transport.h`'s stable-quiescence contract and the implementation comment
that admission/mutation cannot proceed after close.

Independent minimal reproduction against the handed-over source:

```text
clang -Wall -Ianalysis/d113-trace-transport -o /tmp/macpad_d118_closed_test \
  /tmp/macpad_d118_closed_test.c analysis/d113-trace-transport/d113_transport.c -lpthread
/tmp/macpad_d118_closed_test
```

Both commands exited 0; output:

```text
postshutdown: q1=1 close_rc=0 q2=1 reopen_rc=0 q3=1 fd=3
```

The source therefore allows both `d113_drain_close()` and
`d113_drain_open()` after shutdown, while the public predicate remains true.
This is a lifecycle correctness failure, not merely a wording issue. D120
asks for the narrow repair and post-shutdown tests. Scope is limited to the
D113 offline prototype; no production acceptance.

A first disposable harness build exited 139 because it linked
`d113_probe.c` without calling `d113_probe_resolve()`; the probe interposes
libc `open()` and invoked its still-null `RTLD_NEXT` pointer. LLDB showed
`EXC_BAD_ACCESS` at address 0. Rebuilding the minimal harness without that
probe produced the evidence above. Do not treat the initial crash as a
transport result.
